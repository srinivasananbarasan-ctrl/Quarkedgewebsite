Our End-to-End Integration Process

We don't just "mount cameras." We manage the entire machine vision development lifecycle to eliminate execution risk.

Phase 1

Feasibility & Proof of Concept (PoC)

Before you invest, we mitigate your risk. We analyze your parts and environment to guarantee success.

  • Sample Testing & Feasibility Studies: Testing your actual physical parts inside our specialized vision engineering lab environments.
  • Lighting & Optics Selection: Determining exact wavelengths (UV, IR, Polarized) and lens arrays needed.
  • PoC Report: Providing a detailed feasibility report containing real baseline sample images and predictable accuracy limits before hardware orders.
Phase 2

Design & Engineering

Custom hardware layout schemes and software architecture builds tailored explicitly to your target production line parameters.

Mechanical & Electrical Design

Optically stable structural camera brackets, protective environmental enclosures, and complete electrical panel layouts matching industrial schematics rules.

Software Development

Custom UI/UX builds for operator inspection terminals, tracking variables across smart sensors or PC-based vision suites.

Controls Integration

Seamless communication handshakes with native PLCs, field HMIs, and plant-wide MES tracking layers via Profinet, EtherNet/IP, or Modbus networks.

Phase 3

Installation & Commissioning

Mounting hardware components and tuning execution boundaries directly inside active plant assembly blocks.

  • On-Site Hardware Installation: Precision structural positioning of sensors, lens setups, and lighting blocks.
  • Field Calibration: Adjusting operational working distances, aperture settings, and focus rules under real ambient floor reflections.
  • System Tuning: Tuning image execution code directly at standard inline speeds to isolate false-reject failures.
Phase 4

MSA Validation & Compliance

We prove system metrics mathematically before sign-off, leaving zero room for measurement bias or inspection discrepancies.

  • Measurement Systems Analysis (MSA): Executing rigorous Gage R&R studies to ensure camera metrology tool sets score under acceptable variance rules.
  • Attribute Agreement Analysis: Verifying system decision matching limits across pass/fail parameters or deep-learning classifier logic profiles.
  • Validation Protocols (IQ/OQ/PQ): Formally verifying physical placement specs (IQ), performance operating margins (OQ), and reliability under runtime output loads (PQ).
  • 21 CFR Part 11 Compliance: Enabling robust machine audit history, explicit electronic signature tracking, and user tier logins for regulated pharma facilities.

Redesigning Existing Solutions

Stuck with an underperforming system? We evaluate existing legacy configurations, retrofitting optimized industrial lighting setups, proper filtering, or rewriting scripts to transform broken systems into high-yielding assets.

During critical launch windows, we offer dedicated engineering stand-by. We fine-tune parameter thresholds live as speeds scale up, tracking variations across actual production parts to lock down zero-defect validation signs.

Cycle Time Remediation
